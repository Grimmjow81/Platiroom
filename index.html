Welcome to Platiroom

Platiroom is an independent project created to help you track, view, and manage your PlayStation trophy collection locally.

⚠️

Disclaimer: This application has no official affiliation, connection, or endorsement with Sony Interactive Entertainment or PlayStation.

Credits & Acknowledgments

  • Author: Designed and developed by Grimmjow81 with AI assistance.
  • Massicci0 for DB construction and general testing.
  • Special Thanks: to Husky for granting permission to integrate the PSNP+ databases, which allow the app to flag guides, unobtainable trophies, upcoming server shutdowns.

Open Source & Privacy

This project is open-source and free to use. You can view or contribute to the source code on GitHub.

🔒 Your Data is Safe: All your personal data and account details are saved 100% locally on your machine. No personal information is ever transmitted or shared with external servers.
